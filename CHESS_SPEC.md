Audio design on Chess.com provides instant, unambiguous acoustic feedback to keep players informed without interrupting deep cognitive processing. The iconic audio elements include distinct sound cues for:

Standard Move: Light, tactile piece-to-board contact.
Capture: A firmer, heavier impact sound providing immediate visual-auditory parity.
Check & Checkmate: Sharp, distinct alerts signifying elevated game state intensity.
Game Start & Conclusion: Audio cues signaling pairing completion, wins, losses, or draws.
